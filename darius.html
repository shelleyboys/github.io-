Darius’ Basketball Highlights

Football - Fall 2025

One of Darius’ most exciting football moments came when he intercepted a pass and ran it all the way back for a touchdown. It was a game-changer that showed not only his speed and awareness on the field, but also his ability to step up and make big plays when it counts.

Basketball - 3 on 3 - Fall 2025

The 3-on-3 tournaments give Darius the chance to shine in a fast-paced, high-energy setting. With fewer players on the court, every possession mattered, and he shows his ability to handle pressure, create scoring opportunities, and push the tempo. These games are the perfect stage for his competitive drive and love for the game.

About Darius

Even in kindergarten, he was already showing a love for basketball that couldn’t be contained. He practiced layups on the small court, learned to dribble and pass, and quickly picked up the basics of the game. His determination and energy paid off when he earned his very first trophy.

AAO League - Spring-Summer 2025

Darius quickly proved himself as more than just a team player. He became a standout in the AAO Power League. Competing against some of the toughest young athletes in the region, he showed consistency, hustle, and skill that set him apart on the court.
